It is an online 2-player game where players put their ships onto the field during the preparation step. Then they fire in turns to kill another player's ships. The player who fired correctly fires again. The game ends when someone doesn't have any ships.
But I want a modified version where players should put 1 4-cell, 2 3-cell, 3 2-cell, and 4 1-cell ships in addition to the classic version. I want to have one additional 4-cell ship, 1 5-cell ship, 2 turned 4-cell ships, and one ship like 5 in a dice cube. And the field increased to 20x20.
Players have 3 minutes to place ships and 30 seconds for the turn.
If the player didn't put all ships - the rest was placed randomly.
Ships cannot touch each other even diagonally.
if the player didn't fire for 30 seconds - the turn is skipped.
During the game, the player should see two fields. Own with all ships - alive, sunk, and damaged, places where opponent fired and missed.
The opponent field with places where the player has already fired, revealed (damaged and sunk) ships of another player, and places where it doesn't make sense to fire (around sunk ships) are marked with dots
The game should have a great design and atmosphere of a sea battle
It would be great to have sound and animations.
To connect one player creates a room and shares link with another player that joins and when both click ready game starts (preparation step).
